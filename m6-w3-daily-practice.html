Characterization and dialogue
⚖️ THE RULE
Direct characterization names the trait. Indirect characterization shows it through speech, action, looks, or how others react. Dialect should be light, and dialogue needs its marks: quotation marks around the speech, a comma inside them before the tag.
OBJECTION! · NOT GRADED
1. Testimony, claimed as indirect characterization: Mr. Avery was a generous man.
2. Testimony, claimed as indirect characterization: She stepped between the dog and the porch and held her ground.
3. Testimony, claimed as light dialect, correctly punctuated: “Reckon the rain will hold off till supper,” he said.
4. Testimony, claimed as light dialect: “Ah doan reckun we kin cum,” he sed, “fer the rane’s a-comin’ agin.”
