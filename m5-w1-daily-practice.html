Calm or chilling
What a sentence does to the reader
📜 THE RULE
A calm sentence uses plain, pleasant details. A chilling sentence lets one small detail show that something is wrong. Look for the word that does the work.
READ THE SENTENCE · NOT GRADED
1. The morning was clear and sunny, and the families strolled to the square.
2. The old box sat in the corner, and no one wanted to touch it.
Calm or chilling?

Draw a Slip: hints and irony
Two tools for a calm surface
📜 THE RULE
A buried hint hides danger inside a calm sentence. Verbal irony says one thing and means the opposite, and the reader can tell. Draw a slip, do what it says, then write your own below.
📜 DRAW A SLIP
SLIP 1

SLIP 2

SLIP 4

SLIP 5
