Objection! A place that acts
Setting as character, and detail you can sense
⚖️ THE RULE
A setting works as a character when the place acts on the people in it. Sensory detail gives the reader something to see, hear, smell, taste, or touch. A label like scary or old gives the reader nothing to sense.
OBJECTION! · NOT GRADED
1. Testimony, claimed as setting as character: The old house held its breath as the children crept past.
2. Testimony, claimed as setting as character: The house was on the corner, and it was gray.
3. Testimony, claimed as sensory detail: The yard was creepy and old.
4. Testimony, claimed as sensory detail: Red dust caked her ankles, and the porch smelled of wet ash.
