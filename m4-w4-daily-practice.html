Reading the hints
What a detail is warning about
🔥 THE RULE
Foreshadowing plants a hint early about something that will happen later. A hint leaves a mark. It does not tell the event.

2. The hunters act out a kill, laughing and jabbing at a boy who plays the pig. What does it hint at?

What a foil does
Two boys side by side
⚖️ THE RULE
A foil is a character set beside another to sharpen the contrast. Each one shows what the other lacks.
FEED THE FIRE · NOT GRADED
1. One boy keeps the fire and the rules. Another leaves both behind to hunt. What are they to each other?
2. Piggy argues for the rules. Roger throws stones to frighten the small ones. What does the contrast reveal?
3. Why does a writer set two characters side by side?

Signal Fire Sentences: direct and indirect
Say it, or show it
🔥 THE RULE
Direct characterization states the trait outright. Indirect characterization shows it through what the person does, says, or chooses.
FEED THE FIRE · NOT GRADED
1. Piggy was the cleverest boy on the beach. Which kind is this?
2. Jack left the fire to burn low and walked off with the hunters without a backward glance. Which kind is this?
3. Which sentence shows a kind boy indirectly?
