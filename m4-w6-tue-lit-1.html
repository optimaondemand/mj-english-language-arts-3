📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A strong essay starts with one sentence worth defending and a fair list of what could be said against it.
The Module essay answers the central question: when the structures of civilization fall away, what is left of us, the better part or the worse? A thesis is the one-sentence answer, stated so plainly that a reader could disagree with it. The strongest essays do not hide from disagreement. They name the best objection a thoughtful reader could raise and then answer it. In Chapter 10 the boys live with the aftermath of Simon’s death, and the way each of them remembers it is evidence about what is left in them. The three settings, the Beach, Castle Rock, and Simon’s clearing, can each illustrate a claim.
🎯 LESSON OBJECTIVES
1
I will be able to write a clear, arguable thesis and name the strongest objection to it.
2
I will be able to map each of the three island settings to a claim in an essay.

📖 PART 1 · READ, THEN STAKE A CLAIM
⚡ QUICK CHECK-IN
After everything you have read, lean toward an answer. What is left of the boys when civilization falls away?

Read Chapter 10 in your own copy. As you read, mark how Ralph, Piggy, and the others speak about what happened to Simon. Their words are evidence for the argument.

⚡ PART 2 · A THESIS, OR NOT YET A THESIS?

A thesis must take a position someone could argue with. For each sentence below, decide whether it is a real thesis or still needs work.

⚡ TRY IT · IS THIS A THESIS?
Which of these would work as a thesis for your essay?
1. Which sentence is the strongest thesis?
2. Why is “The boys land on an island after a plane crash” not a thesis?
3. Why should your essay name an objection to your own thesis?
🧭 PART 3 · MAP THE THREE SETTINGS TO CLAIMS

Each of the three settings can illustrate a claim in the essay. The map below is a model.

The Beach → the space of order, the conch, and shared rules. Use it to claim: the boys begin with the better part, the will to govern themselves.

Castle Rock → the fortress of fear and force. Use it to claim: when fear rules, the worse part takes the space that order once held.

Simon’s clearing → the quiet place of insight, away from the crowd. Use it to claim: the better part survives, but only in the one who steps apart, and the crowd destroys him.

Each setting is evidence to describe in the essay: point to what Golding places there and explain what it shows about the thesis.

📝 PART 4 · WORD STUDY

A good argument is cogent. Tap to check the meaning.

⚡ TAP TO REVEAL
cogent
Cogent means clear, logical, and convincing. A cogent argument holds together so well that a fair reader has to take it seriously, even if they end up disagreeing.
🌱 WORD ROOTS
Cogent comes from Latin cogere, from co, “together,” and agere, “to drive.” A cogent argument drives the parts together until they compel you. The same agere drives agent and agenda.
📝 WORD STUDY · PRACTICE
Write a sentence describing what makes an essay cogent. Then rewrite it twice: once with a synonym, once expanding it (“cogent because…”). Look cogent up in a dictionary and record three things: how the word breaks into syllables, how it is said aloud, and what part of speech it is.
📝 MEMORY WORK

Drill all three passages and bring the closing lines up to full strength.
