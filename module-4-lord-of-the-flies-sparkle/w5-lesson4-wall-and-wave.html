Answering the objection that fear broke it

Fear is a big part of it, but I don’t think the beast is the whole reason. In Chapter 4 the hunters let the fire go out and a ship passes, and that was about meat and fun, not fear. So the claim still holds, but I’d change it a little: civilization is fragile, and fear makes it break faster.
