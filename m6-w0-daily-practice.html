Stanza and rhyme
ABAB, four times over
⚖️ THE RULE
A stanza is a group of lines set off together. In an ABAB stanza, line 1 rhymes with line 3, and line 2 rhymes with line 4. Give each rhyme its own letter.
ON THE STAND · NOT GRADED
1. Testimony: four lines. The lamp burned low behind the pane / The road ran empty, long and gray / The porch was quiet in the rain / As night came slowly down the way. What is the pattern?
2. In an ABAB stanza, which line rhymes with line 2?
3. In Invictus, which word rhymes with unbowed in stanza 2?
Label each stanza
Write one line for each stanza, such as Stanza 1: A B A B. Use the end-words of the lines to decide.

Objection! Metaphor on the stand
Does the testimony stand?
⚖️ THE RULE
A metaphor calls one thing by the name of another, without saying like or as. If a sentence uses like or as, it is a simile. The testimony below is supposed to be a metaphor.
OBJECTION! · NOT GRADED
1. Testimony: The years ahead were a locked gate he had to push through.
2. Testimony: Trouble was like a heavy coat on his shoulders.
3. Testimony: The shouting was a hammer on the courthouse door.
4. Testimony: Her worry was as dark as a cellar.
