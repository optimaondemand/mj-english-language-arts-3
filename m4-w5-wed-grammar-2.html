Where the Weight Falls
Writing Style Lesson
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A writer decides whether to deliver the point first or save it for last.
Beyond the four sentence shapes, writers control where a sentence puts its weight. A loose sentence states its main idea first, then adds details: The fire went out, abandoned, its smoke thinning to nothing while the boys feasted. The point arrives right away. A periodic sentence holds the main idea until the very end, making the reader wait: Abandoned, its smoke thinning to nothing while the boys feasted, the fire went out. The delay builds suspense and lands the key idea like a closing chord. Neither kind is better. Loose sentences feel natural and clear; periodic sentences feel deliberate and dramatic. Golding uses periodic sentences to make the reader wait through the storm before a death, and loose ones to let action tumble forward.
🎯 LESSON OBJECTIVES
1
I will be able to tell a loose sentence from a periodic sentence.
2
I will be able to turn a loose sentence into a periodic one for effect.
📋 MATERIALS
▶
Writing notebook and pencil. 5 to 10 minutes.
📚 PART 1 · A WORKED EXAMPLE
⚡ QUICK CHECK-IN
A sentence that makes you wait until the last word for its main point. How does that delay feel?

The same idea built two ways, in sentences written for this lesson. The main idea is “the boys fell silent.”

“The boys fell silent, one by one, as the truth of what they had done settled over the dark beach.” → point first, details after. Loose.
“One by one, as the truth of what they had done settled over the dark beach, the boys fell silent.” → details first, point held to the end. Periodic.
⚡ PART 2 · PRACTICE

Each sentence was written for this lesson. Is it loose or periodic?

⚡ TRY IT · LOOSE OR PERIODIC?
Choose where the weight falls.
1. After the heat of the day, after the chant, after the circle had closed in the dark, the storm broke.
2. Ralph ran, stumbling over roots, gasping, hearing the hunters close behind him.
3. In the noise, in the rain, surrounded by the boys he had come to help, Simon died.
4. Piggy held the conch, turning it in his hands, waiting for a silence that would not come.
▶  In your notebook, write a loose sentence, then rewrite it as a periodic one. Then click for an example.
Loose: “The signal fire was dead, its last smoke gone while a ship slipped past the horizon.” Periodic: “Its last smoke gone while a ship slipped past the horizon, the signal fire was dead.” In the periodic version the main point should arrive at the end.
