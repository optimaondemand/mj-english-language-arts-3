🎚️ Tone or Mood?
Two words people mix up
Professor Lovelore Literature expert
0:30
An AI-generated character and voice.
Tonethe attitude in the telling
How the narrator sounds about what he tells you: calm, dry, amazed, mocking.
Moodthe feeling in the reader
The feeling a passage leaves in you: dread, wonder, calm.
They can pull apart
A narrator can sound perfectly steady while you are frightened half to death.
