Jonah’s plan
Jonah · 8th Grade Scholar

World: an ice moon where the snow squeaks and the radio dies on the first day.

Details: snow that squeaks instead of crunching, like a door in an empty house (hear); the smell of warm plastic and my own breath inside the helmet (smell); a slow blue glow far under the ice (see); cold that stings the front teeth when I breathe in (feel); air from the tank that tastes like a coin (taste); a ridge of tall pale shapes that all lean the same way (see).

Mood: uneasy, then full of wonder.

Eyes: Tess, a mapmaker sent alone to survey the moon, who expects empty ground and finds company.

Word: patient, not steady: the glow under the ice is waiting.
