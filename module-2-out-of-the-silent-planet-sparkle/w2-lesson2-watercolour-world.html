protracted

conjured
Called up as if by magic; made to appear in the mind. From the book: “the horrors his imagination had conjured up” (Ch. 8)

(b) Senses: All sight, moving in close: a shape far off, then its movement, then its size, then the light inside the wood.

(c) Details and comparison: Three wrong guesses come first, then the truth. Two details: smooth stalks about forty feet tall, and leaves as big as lifeboats but almost see-through. His comparison: a forest under the sea.

(d) The turn: “Suddenly” his eyes work it out, and puzzlement turns into wonder.
