📜 Make the Case
Why is Earth called the silent planet?
📌 A model case
Jonah
8th Grade Scholar

Claim: Earth is called the silent planet because it is the one world whose ruler never speaks with the rulers of the others.

Clues: Ransom says, “We have no Oyarsa in my world,” and Augray calls that “another proof” it is Thulcandra (Chapter 15). Augray also says an Oyarsa would hardly “never speak to ours.”

Objection: The strongest objection to my reading is that Earth may be silent only because it is so far away, but I still think the silence is about Earth itself, because Augray says Oyarsa “would not think of it like that” (Chapter 15).

Held back: Why does Earth have no Oyarsa, or why does he never speak?

Revise in four moves
  1. Read your opening aloud. Listen for a line that is unclear, or a sense detail that is fuzzy.
  2. Rebuild one paragraph. Choose it from your draft and rewrite it below.
  3. Use the lenses. Imagery, the thing you hold back, gerunds and infinitives, week words.
  4. Show one sentence before and after, and write a short note on what you changed and why.
Your whole story is about 500 to 800 words. You are rebuilding one paragraph of it.
📜 A model revision
Jonah
8th Grade Scholar

Before: The pillars were really cool and kind of glowing.

After: The pillars glowed from inside, the faint pink of a bruise, as if something were thinking behind the ice.

Note: I cut “really cool” because it told my feeling instead of showing the pillars. Now the reader sees the color and wonders what is inside.
