Five lenses for your rewrites
  1. Precision. Swap every vague word for one a reader can see or hear.
  2. Clauses. No dependent clause stands alone.
  3. Adjective clauses. Each one sits beside its noun.
  4. Sound. One phrase whose sound does work.
  5. Week words. Tap any of this week’s ten to hear it, and use one if it fits.
