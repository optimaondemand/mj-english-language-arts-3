Professor Lovelore

Good. Notice how you knew the air was thinning before Lewis said so. A singing in the ears. Cracked lips. No sweat, though he was working hard. A sky going black, with stars in daylight.

Language that lets you see, hear and feel a place is called imagery. Lewis uses it so that you climb the road with Ransom instead of being told about the climb.
