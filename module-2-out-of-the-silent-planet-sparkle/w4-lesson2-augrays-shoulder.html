Big Question
Today’s Wonder

Ransom has feared the sorns since the day he landed, yet one evening by Augray’s fire starts to change his mind. How long does it take to stop seeing a stranger as a monster?

Jonah’s plan
Jonah · 8th Grade Scholar

1. Traveler: Tess, a mapmaker, sent alone to survey an ice moon. Her lander’s radio dies on the first day.

2. First assumption: The tall frost-pillars on the ridge are old machines somebody left behind.

3. The turn: She hums to keep warm, and the nearest pillar changes color in time with her: it is answering.

4. Held back: Why every pillar faces the same dark edge of the sky.

5. Senses: Snow that squeaks instead of crunching; the smell of warm plastic in her helmet; a slow blue glow far under the ice.
