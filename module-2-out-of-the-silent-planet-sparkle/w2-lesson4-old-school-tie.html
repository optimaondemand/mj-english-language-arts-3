Where comma splices hide in your own writing
Professor Lovelore At the board
0:30
An AI-generated character and voice.
Three hiding places
A splice is two complete thoughts with only a comma between them.
One: the long, excited sentence
Ransom ran, he splashed through the water, he climbed the bank.→Ransom ran, splashed through the water, and climbed the bank.
Two: words that only feel like joiners
Ransom ran, then he hid.→Ransom ran, and then he hid.
Three: a comma where none belongs
Ransom ate, and listened.→Ransom ate and listened.
I read all three hiding places.
