caricature
An exaggerated picture that makes something look ridiculous. “… we are giving our readers a mere caricature of the Malacandrian language.”

homogeneous

Yes. Ransom’s letter is a lesson in pacing. A story cannot give you weeks of quiet village life at the speed they were lived. It gives them a sentence, and saves its slow, close scenes for the moments that matter most.

Deciding what to speed through and what to slow down is pacing.

Dear Jonah,

You call the pillars “old machines” in your very first paragraph, before I have even reached the ridge. I did not know what they were, and neither should your reader.
