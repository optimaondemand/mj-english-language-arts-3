2
✏️ Step 1 · Learn
Tap the clause, then answer the question.

A clause has a subject and a verb. An independent clause can stand alone. A dependent clause cannot: it opens with a word that makes it lean on another clause. An adjective clause is a dependent clause that describes a noun.

KindJobExample
independent clausesubject + verb; can stand aloneRansom waited.
dependent clausesubject + verb, but opens with when, because, although, while, if, since, after or untilWhen Ransom waited
adjective clausea dependent clause that describes a noun; opens with who, whom, whose, which or thatthe stone that showed the planets
⚠️

A dependent clause alone is a fragment. When one opens a sentence, a comma follows it. Keep an adjective clause right beside the noun it describes.
