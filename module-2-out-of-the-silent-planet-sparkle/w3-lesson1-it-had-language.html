Sentence: The hross produced an oval platter of some tough but slightly flexible material, covered it with strips of a spongy, orange-coloured substance and gave it to Ransom. (Chapter 9)

One phrase: Of some tough but slightly flexible material is a prepositional phrase. It tells what the platter is made of, and tough but slightly flexible makes me feel it bend in my hand. Lewis spells coloured the British way, so I kept it.
